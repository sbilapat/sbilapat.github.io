About Me

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student on the hunt for a Spring/Summer 2027 internship in Electro-Mechanical Design, Systems Integration, or R&D.

Most of what I do lives at the intersection of hardware and software. I like combining hands-on lab work with data modeling — for example, writing Python simulation scripts to analyze electric vehicle powertrains and map how different factors affect motor efficiency. Whether I'm parsing telemetry data to optimize power delivery or troubleshooting an electrical system that's misbehaving, I get a kick out of finding practical solutions to messy design problems.

Outside the lab, I play football, and it's shaped how I work more than I expected it to. Being on a team where everyone has to execute their role under pressure translates surprisingly well to engineering — you learn to stay level-headed when something breaks mid-project, communicate clearly with the people around you, and push through the reps that aren't glamorous but get the job done.

I'm looking to bring that same combination — technical chops and a competitive drive — to a forward-thinking engineering team. If that sounds like your team, let's talk.
